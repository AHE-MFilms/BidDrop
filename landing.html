How It Works

From First Drive-By to Signed Contract

BidDrop turns every neighborhood you drive through into a source of new business — in 7 steps that take less time than a cold call.

01

You Spot a Property

Driving through a neighborhood, you notice a roof that needs work. You tap the address in BidDrop and drop a pin — takes 5 seconds.

02

BidDrop Pulls the Data

Satellite measurements, roof age, homeowner name, email, and phone — all pulled automatically. No manual research.

03

You Build an Estimate

Select your trade and materials. BidDrop calculates the estimate using real square footage from satellite data. Done in under 2 minutes.

04

A Project Preview Is Built

BidDrop creates a personalized project preview page for the homeowner — with their address, your estimate, your branding, and a QR code.

05

A Postcard Goes in the Mail

You queue a postcard from the field. It's printed and mailed via USPS within 24 hours. The homeowner gets it in 5–7 days.

06

Follow-Up Runs Automatically

BidDrop fires follow-up touchpoints on a schedule — 7, 14, 30 days out. You set it once. Every opportunity stays active without any manual work.

07

You Show Up and Close

By the time you knock, the homeowner has seen your project preview, scanned your QR code, and received your follow-up. You're not cold — you're expected.

08

Storm Leads Find You

BidDrop monitors NOAA MRMS radar daily. When hail hits your territory, you get an alert with the swath on the map — ready to canvass.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
Yes — a credit card is required to start, but you won't be charged a single cent until your first month ends. Cancel anytime before then and you owe nothing.
How does the postcard mailing work?
BidDrop connects to a professional print-and-mail service. When you queue a postcard it gets printed and mailed via USPS — typically arriving within 5–7 business days. Each postcard costs 1 credit ($4 value).
What is Follow-Up Blitz?
Blitz is an automated postcard sequence. You set up 3–5 postcards with different messages, and BidDrop mails them on a schedule you define — 7 days, 14 days, 30 days after the initial knock. It runs automatically without any manual work.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. BidDrop is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — install it on your phone's home screen and it works like a native app, even in areas with spotty signal. The map, pin drops, and estimate builder all work on mobile.
What is GHL integration?
GoHighLevel (GHL) is a popular CRM for contractors. BidDrop's Monthly plan includes a free GHL sub-account — every lead you create in BidDrop automatically appears as a contact in your GHL pipeline, with tags and custom fields. You can also connect your existing CRM.
What is the website embed widget?
Monthly plan users get a widget they can embed on their own website. Homeowners can enter their address and get an instant estimate — without you having to knock the door. It's a 24/7 lead capture tool powered by BidDrop's satellite measurement engine.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. You can change your plan at any time from the Plan & Billing page inside the app. Changes take effect immediately through the Stripe Customer Portal.
