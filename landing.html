Questions

Clear on what happens next.

Does BidDrop replace our CRM?

No. BidDrop helps the field team identify, prepare, and follow up on property opportunities. It can hand the qualified opportunity into your CRM workflow.

What are BidDrop credits used for?

Credits support paid property actions such as mail, measurements, and available homeowner-data workflows. The plan page shows exactly how many monthly credits are included where applicable.

Why are Digital Growth and Omnipresent six-month programs?

Web, content, search visibility, on-site filming, and strategy are planned and delivered across the full six-month engagement rather than treated like a one-time service.

Can I start with software and add marketing later?

Yes. Start with PAYG or BidDrop Pro, then choose a growth program when you are ready for a done-for-you marketing system.
